# Recruiter guide

The AI ranks candidates and shows its evidence. **You make every decision.**

## 1. Add candidates

Your tenant admin sets up the job and question bank. Then: Job → **Candidates** → add one, or import a CSV with columns `name, phone, email, language`
(Indian mobile numbers in any format). Each candidate gets a private interview link.

Click **Invite** to create the link, then share it with **Copy** or **WhatsApp** (it is shown once).
The link works on any phone browser (no app), lasts the number of days set on the job, and can't be
reused after the interview. If someone loses theirs, **Re-invite** gives a new link and stops the old
one; **Revoke** stops it without a replacement.

## 2. What the candidate does (about 15 minutes)

Opens the link → chooses a language → reads the privacy notice and agrees → confirms they are 18+ →
for each question, listens/reads and records an answer (one re-record allowed per question) → done.
If the connection drops, answers already recorded are safe; they reopen the link to continue.

Tell candidates: use Chrome or Safari (not the WhatsApp/Instagram in-app browser; the page explains
how to switch), a quiet place, and allow the microphone.

## 3. Review results

Job → **Results**: candidates ranked by score, with views (All, Needs review, Undecided,
Shortlisted, On hold, Rejected) and filters for language and minimum score. Open an interview to see:

- the overall score and the score per dimension, each with **quotes from the answer** as evidence;
- every answer's recording and transcript;
- flags, e.g. *low transcription confidence* or *knockout answer* (listen to those yourself).

The score is a guide. "Needs review" means the AI was unsure or close to the cut-off.

## 4. Decide

On the interview page: **Shortlist / Hold / Reject**, with a reason. You can **override** any
dimension score (with a reason); the overall score updates and the ranking uses your score. Add
comments for colleagues. Every decision and override is in the activity log.

**Export** (Results page) gives a CSV for your HR system.

## 5. Calibration (before go-live, then monthly)

Job → **Calibration** → **Rate blind**. You see transcripts and recordings, but not the AI score or the
candidate's name. Give 1–5 per dimension and say whether you'd shortlist. When two recruiters
have rated 25–30 interviews, the report shows whether the AI agrees with you well enough:

- AI within 1 point of the recruiter on 80% of ratings;
- the same shortlist decision 85% of the time;
- fewer than 2% of AI quotes not found in the transcript.

If a dimension is consistently off (the report shows "AI vs recruiter" +/−), tell your admin: the
rubric or question bank's model answers may need adjusting.
